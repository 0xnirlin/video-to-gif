How it works

The page runs ffmpeg inside your browser (ffmpeg.wasm, single thread). It scans your frames, picks the 256 colors your clip needs, then draws every frame with that palette. That is the two-pass palette method, and it is why the colors look better than a plain conversion.

Very long or 4K clips can be slow in a browser, and a phone may run out of memory. For those, trim the clip first or use the command line tool.
